# StudentHub

A web-based platform connecting Nigerian tertiary students with SIWES and internship opportunities — built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## About

Finding a SIWES or internship placement in Nigeria often means scrolling through scattered WhatsApp groups, social media posts, and personal contacts. **StudentHub** brings these opportunities together in one searchable, centralized platform.

Students can search and filter opportunities by field of study, location, and opportunity type, view full details for each listing, and save opportunities for later. Organizations can post SIWES and internship opportunities to reach students directly.

## Features

- 🔍 **Search & Filter** — find opportunities by keyword, location (all 36 states + FCT), and field of study
- 📋 **Opportunity Listings** — organization name, location, field, requirements, duration and deadline for each opportunity
- 💾 **Save/Bookmark** — save opportunities to view later
- 📝 **Post an Opportunity** — organizations can submit SIWES/internship listings
- 🔐 **Login & Sign Up** — account pages with password visibility toggle
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — works across mobile, tablet and desktop
- 📄 **About, Contact, Terms of Service & Privacy Policy** pages
