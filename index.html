Our Mission

Connecting Nigerian Students to Real Opportunities

StudentHub exists because finding a SIWES or internship placement shouldn't mean scrolling through endless WhatsApp groups and personal contacts. We bring opportunities together in one organized, searchable place.

500+ Opportunities Listed
200+ Partner Organizations

The Problem

Many Nigerian tertiary students struggle to find SIWES and internship placements because information is scattered across social media, personal networks and individual company websites often leading to missed opportunities and wasted time.

Our Solution

StudentHub centralizes SIWES, internship and future opportunity listings in one platform searchable by field of study, location and opportunity type so students spend less time searching and more time preparing.

Who We're For

Built for Nigerian tertiary students, but also serving the organizations, companies and institutions that want to reach them creating a simple bridge between opportunity and access.
